# Code of conduct

## Scope

This project is for everyone building and running Linux homelabs. Participation
in issues, pull requests, discussions and code review is covered by this policy.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagreement is fine; contempt is not.
- Keep criticism about the code, the design, or the behaviour — not the person.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone else's hostnames, IP addresses, tokens,
  logs, or configuration without permission.
- Help newcomers. A homelab is often someone's first exposure to systemd, RAID,
  containers, or monitoring.
- Accept that maintainers have limited time and may decline a proposal that does
  not fit the project's dependency-free, read-only design.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, threats, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Deliberately derailing issues or repeatedly ignoring maintainer guidance.
- Encouraging or helping others to break the law or compromise systems they do
  not own.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem privately to the maintainer through GitHub's abuse reporting
channel:

<https://github.com/contact/report-abuse>

or contact [@jacoblockard2007-hub](https://github.com/jacoblockard2007-hub)
directly on GitHub. Reports are handled as confidentially as the platform
allows.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, reject contributions,
or block accounts when this policy is violated. Enforcement decisions are made
in good faith and aim to keep the project usable for everyone.
